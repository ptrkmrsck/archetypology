Before You Begin

There is no best way to find your type. A quiz can point, a book can describe, but only you can recognize yourself. You might try reading the portraits and noticing which stack feels familiar, not just the dominant function but the whole arrangement: the way the auxiliary serves the Hero, the way the inferior haunts from below.

Knowing what you aren't is as revealing as knowing what you are. The functions we disown (the inferior sitting in the Anima position, the shadow figures mirroring our strengths from underneath) tell us as much about a type as the functions we claim.

What this quiz does is model the process of placing your stack. It begins with what breaks: the inferior function, which is often easier to recognize than what we lead with. Then it asks about your dominant strengths, distinguishes the introverted and extraverted attitudes of each function, and validates the whole pattern. Think of it as a starting point, not a verdict.
